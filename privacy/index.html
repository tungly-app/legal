Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-24

We built the Tungly app (the "Service") as a tool to help you learn languages. This Service is provided by Isotropy ("we", "us"). You can use Tungly without creating an account; signing in with a Google account is optional and unlocks Cloud Sync as part of the Full Experience subscription. This policy explains what information we collect in either case, where it goes, how long we keep it, and what rights you have over it.

The short version. Your cards live on your device by default. Words and phrases you look up are sent to Google's Gemini AI for translation, and the result — including the text you entered — is stored in a shared collection that other Tungly users draw on, without any link to you. If you sign in, your learning progress also syncs to the cloud under your account. We show ads to users without a subscription. We do not sell your data.

1. Information We Collect and Use

A. Account Information (Optional — Firebase Authentication / Google Sign-In)

Tungly does not require an account to use. If you have an active Full Experience subscription, you may choose to sign in with a Google account to enable Cloud Sync. When you sign in, Google provides us with:

  • Your Firebase UID — a unique identifier for your account within the app.
  • Your email address and display name as registered with your Google account.

This information is handled by Firebase Authentication (Google) and is used to associate your synced learning content with your account. Firebase Authentication may hold additional profile information from your Google account (such as your profile photo URL) on Google's servers as part of maintaining the sign-in record, even where the app itself does not read or display it.

If your Full Experience subscription ends, the app automatically signs you out and stops syncing your cards. Your data already in the cloud is not deleted when this happens — see section 3.

B. Learning Content You Create

How your cards are stored depends on whether you are signed in:

  • Without sign-in (default): Your cards and learning progress are stored only on your device, in a local database. They are not associated with any account on our servers, and uninstalling the app removes them.
  • While signed in (Cloud Sync, Full Experience only): Your learning progress is also saved to Google Cloud Firestore under your Firebase UID. These records consist of references to entries in the shared collection described below, together with your progress data: your favourite and known flags, your sequential-known count, the number of entries you have imported, and the timestamps for when you added and last interacted with each card. Sync happens both immediately when you create or update a card and periodically in the background (roughly every six hours) via Android WorkManager, including when the app is not open.

Shared translation collection — please read this carefully. When you look up a word or phrase that no one has looked up before in that language pair, we send it to Google's Gemini AI (see section C) and then store the result — including the original text you entered — in a collection in Cloud Firestore that is shared across all Tungly users. When another user later enters the same word in the same language pair, they are served your stored entry instead of us re-running the AI. This is what keeps the app fast and affordable to run.

  • The shared entry is keyed by the language pair and the input text only. It is not linked to your account, device, IP address, or any other identifier, and it carries nothing that would let us or anyone else trace it back to you.
  • This happens whether or not you are signed in, and on both the free and paid tiers.
  • Entries in the shared collection are retained indefinitely and are readable by other users of the app.
  • Because of this, please do not enter personal information — names, addresses, account numbers, health details, or anything else private — into the app. Tungly is a vocabulary tool; treat what you type as public.

If you believe a shared entry contains personal information or content that should not be there, email us at tungly2025@gmail.com with the word or phrase and the language pair, and we will remove it. Because these entries carry no identifier, we cannot locate them from your account alone — we need the text itself.

C. AI Processing (Google Gemini via Firebase AI)

When you enter a word or phrase for analysis, that text and your target language code are sent to Google's Gemini API (via Firebase AI Logic, using the Gemini Developer API) for processing. The model currently used is a Gemini Flash model. We do not send your account UID, email, or any other personal identifier alongside this request. The text is used to generate the learning card you requested, and the generated result is then stored in the shared collection described in section B.

D. Diagnostics and Usage (Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics)

To help us identify bugs and improve the app, we use:

  • Firebase Crashlytics: If the app crashes or encounters an error, a crash report is sent to Firebase Crashlytics. This includes the error and stack trace, your device model, operating system version, app version, and your IP address. It also includes a short trail of recent in-app diagnostic messages leading up to the error. Those diagnostic messages may include the word or phrase being processed at the time, along with your Firebase UID and your subscription status. We are working to remove user-entered text from these reports; until then, we would rather tell you it is there than claim otherwise.
  • Firebase Analytics: We log in-app events — such as sync successes and failures, settings changes, starting an import, generating a PDF export, and counts of how many translations came from the shared collection versus the AI. We also set user properties recording whether you have an active Full Experience subscription and whether you are signed in. Firebase Analytics additionally collects certain information automatically, including an app instance identifier, device and operating system details, and an approximate location derived from your IP address.

Analytics events record counts and labels only — the words and phrases you enter are not included in them. If you are signed in, these reports are tagged with your Firebase UID; if you are not signed in, they are tagged as anonymous. Both services are provided by Google.

E. Subscriptions and Payments (RevenueCat and Google Play)

We do not process payments directly. All transactions are handled securely by Google Play, and we never receive your card or billing details.

  • RevenueCat: We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status. RevenueCat is configured to use an anonymous device identifier — we do not pass your Firebase UID, email, or name to RevenueCat. It receives your purchase and subscription history for the app in order to tell us whether your subscription is active.

F. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Tungly is free to use, and we cover part of the cost by showing ads. If you do not have an active Full Experience subscription, the app may display banner advertising supplied by Google AdMob. A Full Experience subscription removes all advertising.

  • The advertising SDK is initialised when the app starts, and may collect your Advertising ID (a resettable identifier provided by Android), your IP address, device and operating system information, and information about your interaction with ads.
  • The app declares the Android Privacy Sandbox permissions (Topics and Attribution) used by Google's advertising libraries.
  • Ads may be personalised based on this information. Google acts as an independent controller for the advertising data it collects; see how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, and turn off ad personalisation, in your Android device settings under Settings → Privacy → Ads. You can remove advertising from the app entirely by subscribing to Full Experience.

We do not sell your personal information. Where personalised advertising counts as "sharing" or a "sale" under laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act, see section 4.

4. Your Rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, or delete it, wherever you live, by emailing tungly2025@gmail.com. We do not charge for this and we will not treat you differently for asking. Some additional rights apply depending on where you are.

Isotropy is based in Canada. Under the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act you have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, to ask us to correct it, and to challenge our handling of it. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

If you are a Quebec resident, Law 25 additionally gives you the right to receive your data in a structured, commonly used technological format, and to ask us to stop disseminating personal information or to de-index it where its dissemination causes you injury. Our privacy officer can be reached at the contact details in section 7.
